## 🏍️ **Step 2: Understanding Event Types**

### **Chapter Rides 🏍️**

**What**: Group motorcycle rides to destinations around Florida
**Frequency**: 1-2 times per month (typically weekends)
**Duration**: 2-6 hours (varies by route)
**RSVP Required**: YES (helps Road Captain plan route and stops)

**Typical Schedule:**
- **9:00 AM** - Arrive at meeting point (parking lot, gas station)
- **9:15 AM** - Safety briefing by Road Captain
- **9:30 AM** - Ride departs (staggered formation)
- **12:00 PM** - Lunch stop destination
- **2:00 PM** - Return ride begins
- **3:30 PM** - Arrive back at meeting point

**What to Bring:**
- Motorcycle (properly maintained, full tank of gas)
- Riding gear (helmet, gloves, jacket, boots)
- Water bottle (stay hydrated!)
- Snacks (energy bars for long rides)
- Cash (for lunch stop or gas)
- Cell phone (emergency contact)

**RSVP Etiquette:**
- RSVP at least 24 hours before ride
- If you RSVP Yes but can't attend, update RSVP ASAP (Road Captain counts on your response)
- Arrive 15 minutes early for safety briefing

---

### **Chapter Meetings 📋**

**What**: Monthly business meeting for all members
**Frequency**: Monthly (typically first Thursday at 7:00 PM)
**Duration**: 1-2 hours
**RSVP Recommended**: Yes (helps plan seating and refreshments)

**Typical Agenda:**

**What to Bring:**
- CVMA vest/colors (if you have patch)

- Dues payment (if renewal due)

**RSVP Etiquette:**
- RSVP helps chapter plan food/drinks

- Arrive on time (meeting starts promptly)

## ✅ **Step 3: How to RSVP for an Event**

### **Standard RSVP Process:**

**1. Find Event on Calendar**
- Navigate to calendar (Events menu)
- Click event name to open details page

**2. Review Event Information**
- **Event Name**: "September Chapter Ride - St. Augustine"
- **Date & Time**: Saturday, September 15, 2025 - 9:00 AM
- **Meeting Point**: Wawa Gas Station, I-95 Exit 318
- **Destination**: St. Augustine Historic District
- **Distance**: 120 miles round trip
- **Organizer**: Road Captain John Doe
- **Description**: Scenic coastal ride with lunch stop at historic restaurant
- **Current RSVPs**: 12 members attending

**3. Choose Your RSVP Response**

**🟢 Attending (Yes)**
- Click green **RSVP Yes** button
- You will attend this event
- Organizer counts on your participation
- Your name added to attendee list

**🟡 Maybe**
- Click yellow **RSVP Maybe** button
- You're interested but not 100% committed
- Organizer knows you're considering
- Update to Yes/No when you decide

**🔴 Not Attending (No)**
- Click red **RSVP No** button (optional)
- Polite way to decline invitation
- Not required (simply don't RSVP Yes)
- Your name removed from attendee list if previously RSVP'd Yes

**4. Confirm Your RSVP**
- Confirmation message displays: "You are attending [Event Name]"
- Email confirmation sent to your inbox
- Event added to "My Events" page

**5. Add to Personal Calendar** (Optional but Recommended)
- Click **Add to Calendar** button
- Choose calendar app:
  - Google Calendar
  - Outlook Calendar
  - Apple Calendar (iPhone/Mac)
- Event syncs to your personal calendar with automatic reminder

---

### **Bringing a Guest:**

**For Events That Allow Guests:**

1. After RSVP'ing "Yes", click **Add Guest** button
2. Enter guest information:
   - **Guest Name**: Full name
   - **Guest Email**: Email address (optional)
   - **Guest Relationship**: Spouse, Friend, Family, etc.
3. Click **Save Guest**
4. Guest receives event details email (if email provided)
5. Guest count updated on event page

**Guest Policy by Event Type:**
- **Rides**: Guests must have own motorcycle + gear (Road Captain approval)
- **Meetings**: CVMA members only (guests require special CEB vote)
- **Social Events**: Spouses, family, friends welcome
- **Memorial Events**: Check event description (sometimes members-only)

**Important**: YOU are responsible for your guest's conduct. If your guest violates CVMA rules or disturbs event, you may face disciplinary action.

## 📱 **Step 4: Manage Your RSVPs**

### **View Your Events:**

1. Click **My Events** in navigation menu
2. See all events you've RSVP'd for
3. Filter options:
   - **Upcoming**: Events in the future
   - **Past**: Events you've attended
   - **RSVP'd Yes**: Events you confirmed attendance
   - **Maybe**: Events you're considering

---

### **Change Your RSVP:**

**Scenario**: You RSVP'd Yes for Saturday ride, but work called you in

**Steps to Update:**
1. Navigate to **My Events**
2. Click event name
3. Click **Update RSVP** button
4. Select new response:
   - **Yes** → **Maybe** (if unsure)
   - **Yes** → **No** (if definitely can't attend)
5. Click **Save**
6. Organizer receives notification of RSVP change

**RSVP Change Etiquette:**
- Update ASAP (don't wait until day-of)
- Especially important for rides (Road Captain needs accurate count)
- Especially important for meals (food orders placed 24-48 hours early)
- No judgment - life happens, just let organizer know!

---

### **Cancel RSVP Completely:**

**Steps:**
1. Open event page
2. Click **Cancel RSVP** button
3. Confirm cancellation: "Are you sure?" → **Yes, Cancel**
4. Removed from attendee list
5. Organizer notified

**When to Cancel vs Update to "No":**
- **Cancel**: You initially RSVP'd but now can't attend
- **No**: You're declining an invitation (never said Yes)

## 🌦️ **Step 6: Weather Cancellations & Event Changes**

### **Who Decides to Cancel:**

**Rides**: Road Captain decides (safety first!)
- Heavy rain forecasted: Likely canceled
- Scattered showers: Ride may proceed (rain gear recommended)
- Severe weather (storms, high winds): Definitely canceled

**Meetings**: Commander decides
- Typically NOT canceled (indoor event)
- Exception: Hurricane, severe emergency

**Social Events**: Event Organizer decides
- Outdoor events may be rescheduled for weather
- Indoor events typically proceed

---

### **How You'll Be Notified:**

**Cancellation Timeline:**
- **24+ hours notice**: Email + portal notification
- **Day-of cancellation**: Email + text message (if phone on file)
- **Last-minute cancellation**: Phone call or group text

**Check Before You Leave:**
1. Log into portal or check email
2. Look for event updates or cancellation notices
3. When in doubt, contact organizer (listed on event page)

**Rescheduled Events:**
- New event created with updated date/time
- Original RSVP does NOT carry over
- You must RSVP again for rescheduled event

## 🎯 **Step 7: Event Etiquette & Best Practices**

### **RSVP Etiquette:**

**DO:**
- ✅ RSVP at least 24-48 hours before event
- ✅ Update RSVP immediately if plans change
- ✅ Arrive on time (or early for rides)
- ✅ Bring what event description lists
- ✅ Thank organizer after event
- ✅ Offer to help (setup/cleanup always appreciated)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Ghost (RSVP Yes then don't show without notice)
- ❌ Bring uninvited guests (always ask first)
- ❌ Show up drunk or intoxicated
- ❌ Complain about free food/events (volunteers worked hard!)
- ❌ Leave early without telling organizer
- ❌ No-call/no-show (especially for rides - safety concern!)

---

### **Ride-Specific Etiquette:**

**Before Ride:**
- ✅ Full tank of gas (don't make group wait for you to fuel up)
- ✅ Motorcycle properly maintained (check tires, brakes, lights)
- ✅ Arrive 15 minutes early for safety briefing
- ✅ Use bathroom before departure (minimize stops)

**During Ride:**
- ✅ Follow Road Captain's hand signals
- ✅ Ride in staggered formation (as instructed)
- ✅ Maintain safe following distance
- ✅ No lane splitting or dangerous maneuvers
- ✅ Stay with group (don't ride ahead or lag behind)
- ✅ If breakdown occurs, pull over safely and signal group

**After Ride:**
- ✅ Thank Road Captain for leading
- ✅ Report any safety concerns
- ✅ Offer feedback (favorite stops, route suggestions)

---

### **Meeting Etiquette:**

**DO:**
- ✅ Silence cell phone before meeting starts
- ✅ Wait to be recognized by Commander before speaking
- ✅ Respect differing opinions during votes
- ✅ Stay for entire meeting (leaving early is disruptive)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Side conversations during CEB reports
- ❌ Texting during meeting
- ❌ Interrupting speakers
- ❌ Arguing or being disrespectful

## 🚨 **Troubleshooting Common Issues**

### **Issue 1: Can't Find Event on Calendar**

**Possible Causes:**
- Event in future month (check date filter)
- Event past (check "Past Events")
- Event canceled/deleted

**Resolution:**
1. Clear date filters ("All Events")
2. Use search box: Enter event name or keyword
3. Check "My Events" if you RSVP'd
4. Contact Secretary if event should exist but doesn't appear

---

### **Issue 2: RSVP Button Grayed Out (Can't Click)**

**Possible Causes:**
- Event already happened (past date)
- Event at capacity (full roster)
- RSVP deadline passed
- You're already RSVP'd Yes

**Resolution:**
- **If event full:** Contact organizer to be added to waitlist
- **If deadline passed:** Contact organizer directly to request late RSVP
- **If already RSVP'd:** Look for "Update RSVP" button instead

---

### **Issue 3: Didn't Receive RSVP Confirmation Email**

**Possible Causes:**
- Email in spam/junk folder
- Wrong email address on profile
- Email notifications disabled

**Resolution:**
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Profile → Verify email address correct
3. Profile → Settings → Notifications → Enable event notifications
4. Check "My Events" to confirm RSVP recorded (even without email)

---

### **Issue 4: Added to Event But Didn't RSVP**

**Possible Causes:**
- Organizer manually added you (assuming attendance)
- Auto-enrollment for mandatory events (chapter meetings)

**Resolution:**
- Check email for organizer message explaining why added
- If don't want to attend, RSVP "No" or contact organizer
- Mandatory events (official meetings): attendance required for Full Members

---

### **Issue 5: Can't Bring Guest (No "Add Guest" Button)**

**Possible Causes:**
- Event doesn't allow guests (members-only)
- Guest limit reached (max guests per member)
- Event at capacity (no room for guests)

**Resolution:**
- Read event description: "Guests Allowed: Yes/No"
- Contact organizer to request exception
- If members-only, guests cannot attend (Bylaws restriction)
