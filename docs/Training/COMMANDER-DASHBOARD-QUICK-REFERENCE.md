# Commander Dashboard - Quick Reference Guide
**CVMA Chapter 20-7 - Combat Veterans Motorcycle Association**
**Executive Summary for Chapter Leadership**

## 🎯 **Dashboard Purpose**

The Commander Dashboard gives you **chapter health at a glance** - everything you need to lead effectively in one place.

**What You See:**
- Real-time membership metrics
- Financial health indicators
- Event participation analytics
- CEB performance tracking

**Access:** Click **Commander Dashboard** tile on home page (or Apps → Commander Dashboard)

---

## 📊 **Dashboard Overview (Top to Bottom)**

### **Section 1: Chapter Overview** (Top Row)

**Total Active Members**
- Current member count (Full, Associate, Supporter)
- Trend vs last month (up/down arrow)
- **Action Needed**: If declining, discuss retention at CEB meeting

**New Members This Month**
- Members who joined this month
- Compare to recruitment goals
- **Action Needed**: Welcome new members personally

**Member Retention Rate**
- Percentage of members renewing (rolling 12 months)
- **Target**: 85%+ retention
- **Action Needed**: If <80%, identify why members leaving

**CEB Position Vacancies**
- Open CEB officer positions
- **Action Needed**: If vacant >30 days, recruit or appoint interim

---

### **Section 2: Financial Health** (Middle Left)

**Monthly Revenue**
- Total income this month (dues + donations + fundraisers)
- **Action Needed**: If trending down, plan fundraisers

**Monthly Expenses**
- Total spending this month
- **Action Needed**: If exceeds revenue, review budget with Treasurer

**Net Cash Position**
- Chapter bank balance RIGHT NOW
- **Color Code**:
  - 🟢 Green: >$5,000 (healthy)
  - 🟡 Yellow: $2,000-$5,000 (caution)
  - 🔴 Red: <$2,000 (emergency - freeze spending)

**Budget Variance Alert**
- Over/under budget indicator
- **Action Needed**: If red, discuss budget adjustment with Treasurer

---

### **Section 3: Event Participation** (Middle Right)

**Upcoming Events** (Next 30 days)
- Count of scheduled events
- **Action Needed**: If <2 events/month, plan more activities

**Average RSVP Rate**
- Percentage of members RSVP'ing for events
- **Target**: 40%+ RSVP rate
- **Action Needed**: If <30%, boost communication or event appeal

**Top Attended Events** (Year-to-date)
- Which events members love most
- **Action Needed**: Plan similar events (what works, do more!)

**Safety Incidents**
- Ride accidents or safety issues (from Road Captain)
- **Target**: ZERO incidents
- **Action Needed**: If >0, review safety protocols

---

### **Section 4: CEB Performance** (Bottom)

**Term Expiration Timeline**
- Which CEB officers' terms ending soon
- **Action Needed**: 90 days before expiration, plan elections (User Story #67)

**Administrative Hold Active Count**
- Members under disciplinary investigation
- **Action Needed**: Monitor 90-day deadline compliance (User Story #68)

**Outstanding Action Items**
- Tasks assigned to CEB officers (from meetings)
- **Action Needed**: Follow up on overdue tasks

**Last CEB Meeting Date**
- When last CEB meeting held
- **Action Needed**: If >45 days ago, schedule meeting ASAP

## 🎯 **Monthly CEB Meeting Presentation**

### **Use Dashboard to Brief CEB:**

**1. Open Commander Dashboard Before Meeting**
- Review all metrics
- Screenshot dashboard (or display on screen during meeting)

**2. Present Each Section:**

**"Membership Update"** (Section 1)
- "We have [X] active members, up/down [X] from last month"
- "We welcomed [X] new members this month"
- "Our retention rate is [X]% - target is 85%"

**"Financial Health"** (Section 2)
- "Monthly revenue was $[X], expenses $[X]"
- "We have $[X] in the bank - we're in [good/caution/critical] shape"
- "Treasurer will provide detailed report next"

**"Event Participation"** (Section 3)
- "We have [X] events planned for next month"
- "[X]% of members are RSVP'ing for events"
- "Our most popular events were [list top 3]"
- "Road Captain reports [X] safety incidents - [discuss if >0]"

**"CEB Status"** (Section 4)
- "[Officer names] terms expiring in next 90 days - need elections"
- "[X] members under Administrative Hold - all on track for 90-day resolution"
- "[X] outstanding action items from last meeting - assignments updated"

**3. Dashboard Questions CEB May Ask:**

**Q: "Why did expenses spike this month?"**
**A:** Click "Monthly Expenses" → Drill down to expense breakdown → Show category (e.g., "Fall BBQ food costs")

**Q: "Who are the new members?"**
**A:** Click "New Members This Month" → Shows list of names → "Welcome [names] - let's introduce them at next meeting"

**Q: "Which events had poor turnout?"**
**A:** Click "Top Attended Events" → Sort ascending (lowest first) → Identify low-turnout events

## 🔍 **Drill-Down: How to Investigate Metrics**

### **Click Any Number to See Details:**

**Example: "Total Active Members" shows 48**
1. Click "48" (blue hyperlink)
2. Report opens showing all 48 members
3. Filter by:
   - Membership Level (Full, Associate, Supporter)
   - Dues Status (Current, Expiring Soon, Past Due)
   - Last Event Attended (find inactive members)
4. Export list to Excel if needed

**Example: "Net Cash Position" shows $3,200 (Yellow)**
1. Click "$3,200" hyperlink
2. Shows all transactions this month
3. Identify largest expenses
4. Discuss with Treasurer: "Can we delay [expense] until next month?"

## 💰 **Financial Health Interpretation**

### **Green Status ($5,000+):**
- ✅ Chapter financially healthy
- ✅ Can afford planned events
- ✅ Veteran assistance fund available
- **Action**: Maintain current operations

### **Yellow Status ($2,000-$5,000):**
- ⚠️ Caution zone - monitor closely
- ⚠️ Delay non-essential spending
- ⚠️ Plan fundraiser within 60 days
- **Action**: Treasurer presents recovery plan at CEB meeting

### **Red Status (<$2,000):**
- 🔴 Emergency - freeze non-essential spending
- 🔴 Postpone events with high costs
- 🔴 Immediate fundraiser required
- 🔴 No veteran assistance payments until balance recovers
- **Action**: Emergency CEB meeting to address crisis

**Daily** (5 minutes):

- Review membership trends

## 🚨 **Alert Thresholds (When to Take Action)**

**Membership Alerts:**
- 🔴 **New Members <2/month** for 3 months straight → Boost recruitment efforts
- 🔴 **Retention Rate <75%** → Investigate why members leaving (exit surveys)
- 🔴 **CEB Vacancy >30 days** → Appoint interim officer ASAP

**Financial Alerts:**
- 🔴 **Cash Balance <$2,000** → Emergency CEB meeting
- 🟡 **Monthly Expenses >Revenue** for 2 months → Budget adjustment needed
- 🔴 **Past Due Dues >20% of members** → Collections effort required

**Event Alerts:**
- 🟡 **RSVP Rate <30%** → Events not appealing or poor communication
- 🔴 **Safety Incidents >0** → Immediate safety review with Road Captain
- 🟡 **No Events Planned Next Month** → Schedule rides/activities ASAP

**CEB Alerts:**
- 🔴 **CEB Term Expiring <90 days** → Begin election process (Bylaws requirement)
- 🔴 **Administrative Hold >75 days** → Investigation deadline approaching
- 🟡 **Outstanding Tasks >10** → CEB officers overloaded (redistribute)
