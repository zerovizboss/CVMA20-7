# Secretary Documentation Management Guide
**CVMA Chapter 20-7 - Combat Veterans Motorcycle Association**
**Document Management & Communication for Chapter Secretary**

## 📋 **Secretary Role Overview**

As Chapter Secretary, you're the keeper of chapter records, communications coordinator, and documentation manager. This guide helps you manage documents, meeting minutes, and member communications effectively.

**Your Responsibilities:**
- ✅ Maintain chapter bylaws and important documents
- ✅ Record and distribute meeting minutes
- ✅ Coordinate member communications
- ✅ Manage member portal access and support
- ✅ Track correspondence and chapter records

**Estimated Time:** 2-3 hours/week for typical Secretary duties

2. **Create New Folder** (if needed)

3. **Upload File**

**Step 2: Type Up Minutes After Meeting**

1. **Use Template Above** - Fill in all sections
2. **Proofread** - Check spelling, grammar, accuracy
3. **Save as PDF**: "Chapter-Meeting-Minutes-September-2025.pdf"
4. **Get Commander Approval** - Email draft to Commander for review
5. **Commander Reviews** - Makes edits/corrections, approves

---

**Step 3: Distribute Minutes**

1. **Upload to Member Portal**
   - **Files** → "Meeting Minutes" folder
   - Upload approved PDF
   - Set permissions: "All Members"

2. **Email to All Members**
   - Subject: "Chapter Meeting Minutes - September 2025"
   - Body:
     ```

     Attached are the minutes from our September chapter meeting.

     Key Highlights:
     - [Bullet 1 - major vote or decision]
     - [Bullet 2 - upcoming event]
     - [Bullet 3 - important announcement]

     Next Meeting: [Date, Time, Location]

     [Your Name]
     ```

   - Send to "All Members" email list

3. **Present at Next Meeting**
   - Bring printed copies to next meeting
   - Read aloud brief summary
   - Ask for corrections/additions
   - Vote to approve minutes from last meeting
   - Once approved, mark PDF "APPROVED" and re-upload

### **CEB Meeting Minutes** (Restricted)

**Similar Process, BUT:**
- **Not all CEB discussions are public** (disciplinary matters, sensitive member issues)
- **Create TWO Versions**:
  1. **Full Minutes** (CEB Officers Only) - Complete record including confidential discussions
  2. **Public Summary** (All Members) - Redacted version removing sensitive info

**Example Redaction:**
- **Full Version**: "CEB voted to place Member John Doe on Administrative Hold for 90 days pending investigation of Bylaws violation Article III.02"
- **Public Version**: "CEB discussed member conduct matter - investigation ongoing per Bylaws Appendix C"

**Storage:**
- Full Minutes → **Files** → "CEB Minutes" folder → **Permissions: CEB Officers Only**
- Public Summary → **Files** → "Meeting Minutes" folder → **Permissions: All Members**

## 🔍 **Common Secretary Tasks**

### **Task 1: Add New Member to Portal**

**Scenario:** Member application approved, need to create portal login

1. **Create Contact Record** (if doesn't exist)
   - **Contacts** tab → **New**
   - Fill in: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone
   - **Membership Level**: Supporter, Associate, or Full Member
   - **Member Since**: Today's date
   - **Status**: Active
   - Click **Save**

2. **Create User Account** (Portal Login)
   - On Contact record, click **New User** button
   - **Username**: Member's email address
   - **User License**: Customer Community Plus (or applicable license)
   - **Profile**: CVMA Member Profile
   - **Permission Sets**: Assign applicable permission sets
   - **Generate Password**: Yes (temporary password)
   - Click **Save**

3. **Send Welcome Email**
   - System automatically sends username + temporary password to member
   - Member receives "Welcome to CVMA Chapter 20-7 Portal" email
   - Member logs in, changes password on first login

4. **Follow Up**
   - Call member 2-3 days later: "Did you get the welcome email? Any trouble logging in?"
   - Troubleshoot login issues if needed
   - Confirm member completed profile

### **Task 4: Process Membership Transfer Request**

**Scenario:** Member moving to another state, wants to transfer to different chapter

1. **Member Submits Form 101** (Membership Transfer Request)
   - Member downloads Form 101 from document library
   - Completes form, emails to you

2. **Verify Transfer Eligibility**
   - Check National Bylaws Article IV: Member must be in good standing
   - Check dues status: Must be current (no outstanding dues)
   - Check disciplinary status: Cannot transfer if on Administrative Hold

3. **Update Contact Record**
   - Edit member Contact
   - **Membership Status**: Transferred (not Active, not Inactive)
   - **Transfer Date**: Today's date
   - **Transfer To Chapter**: [New chapter number and location]
   - **Notes**: "Transferred to Chapter [X] per Form 101 on [date]"
   - Click **Save**

4. **Notify Receiving Chapter**
   - Email receiving chapter's Secretary
   - Subject: "Membership Transfer - [Member Name] from Chapter 20-7"
   - Attach: Member's Form 101, proof of dues current
   - CC: Member (so they have contact info for new chapter)

5. **Update Chapter Records**
   - Member count decreases by 1
   - Record transfer in monthly meeting minutes
   - Archive member file (don't delete - maintain history)

## 📞 **Member Support (Portal Issues)**

### **Common Member Questions:**

**Q: "I forgot my password, how do I reset it?"**
**A:**
1. Member clicks "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enters email address
3. Receives password reset email
4. Clicks link in email, creates new password
5. If doesn't receive email, check spam folder
6. If still issues, you can manually reset: Contact record → Click **Reset Password** button

**Q: "I can't access [Document/Page], it says 'Insufficient Privileges'"**
**A:**
1. Check member's permission sets: Contact → Permission Set Assignments
2. Verify member has appropriate permission set for that resource
3. If missing, assign permission set
4. Ask member to log out, wait 5 minutes, log back in
5. Permission should now grant access

**Q: "I want to update my profile photo, how do I do that?"**
**A:**
1. Direct member to: Profile → My Profile
2. Click **Upload Photo** button
3. Select photo from device (JPG or PNG, max 2 MB)
4. Crop photo if needed
5. Click **Save**
6. Photo appears within 5 minutes

**Q: "I'm not receiving email notifications for events"**
**A:**
1. Check member's email address on Contact record (correct?)
2. Check member's notification settings: Profile → Settings → Notifications
3. Verify event notification boxes checked
4. Check spam/junk folder (CVMA emails may be filtered)
5. Add noreply@cvma20-7.org to contacts (whitelist sender)

## 🔐 **CEB-Restricted Content Management**

### **Documents CEB Officers Only:**

Some documents should NOT be visible to all members:
- CEB meeting minutes (full version with confidential discussions)
- Disciplinary investigation files (Appendix C proceedings)
- Financial audit reports (sensitive chapter financial data)
- Member disciplinary records
- Legal correspondence

**How to Restrict Document Access:**

1. **Upload Document** to appropriate folder
2. **Set Permission**:
   - Click document → **Share** button
   - **Share With**: "CEB Officers" (not "All Members")
   - **Access Level**: Viewer (not Editor)
   - Click **Share**

3. **Verify Restriction**:
   - Log out
   - Log in as regular member test account
   - Try to access document
   - Should see "You don't have access to this file"

4. **CEB Officer Access**:
   - CEB officers with CVMA_Commander_Access, CVMA_Secretary_Access, etc. permission sets can view
   - Guest users and regular members cannot view

## 💡 **Secretary Pro Tips**

**Document Organization:**
- Use consistent naming convention: "CVMA-[DocumentType]-[Name]-[Version].pdf"
- Examples:
  - CVMA-Form-101-Transfer-Request.pdf
  - CVMA-Bylaws-Chapter-20-7-v3.pdf
  - CVMA-Minutes-September-2025.pdf
- Makes searching and archiving easier!

**Meeting Minutes Efficiency:**
- Bring laptop to meeting (type notes in real-time)
- Use meeting minutes template (copy/paste, fill in blanks)
- Record meeting on phone (with CEB permission) to verify details later
- Draft minutes within 24 hours while meeting fresh in mind

**Communication Best Practices:**
- Reply to member emails within 24 hours (even if just "I received your message, will respond fully by [date]")
- Keep CEB officers CC'd on important correspondence
- File correspondence in folders (not just inbox chaos!)
- Use Chatter for quick announcements (faster than mass email)

**Member Support:**
- Create FAQ document (Common questions + answers)
- Post FAQ in document library (reduces repeat questions)
- Maintain "New Member Onboarding Checklist" (ensure consistent welcome process)
