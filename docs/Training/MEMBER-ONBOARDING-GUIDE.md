## 🎖️ **Welcome, New Member!**

Congratulations on joining the Combat Veterans Motorcycle Association (CVMA) Chapter 20-7! This guide will help you get started with our member portal and connect you with your brothers and sisters in the CVMA community.

**What You'll Learn:**
- How to access your member portal
- Setting up your member profile
- RSVP'ing for chapter events
- Accessing bylaws and important documents

**Estimated Time:** 15-20 minutes to complete initial setup

## 🔐 **Step 1: Access Your Member Portal (First-Time Login)**

### **What You Received:**

After your membership application was approved, you should have received:
1. **Welcome Email** from Chapter Secretary with your login credentials
2. **Member Portal URL:** https://cvma20-7.force.com (or similar)
3. **Temporary Password** (you'll change this on first login)

### **First-Time Login Process:**

1. **Open Your Browser**
   - Use Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (mobile browsers work too!)
   - Navigate to the member portal URL from your welcome email

2. **Enter Your Credentials**
   - **Username:** Your email address (e.g., yourname@example.com)
   - **Password:** Temporary password from welcome email
   - Click **Log In**

3. **Reset Your Password** (required on first login)
   - Enter temporary password
   - Create new strong password:
     - At least 8 characters
     - Include uppercase and lowercase letters
     - Include at least one number
     - Include at least one special character (!, @, #, $, etc.)
   - Confirm new password
   - Click **Reset Password**

4. **Security Question Setup** (if prompted)
   - Choose security question
   - Provide answer you'll remember
   - This helps you recover your account if you forget your password

5. **Welcome to Your Member Portal!**
   - You should now see the CVMA Chapter 20-7 home page
   - Military ribbon-styled navigation bar at top
   - Chapter news and upcoming events displayed

---

### **Troubleshooting First-Time Login:**

**Problem: "Invalid username or password"**
- Double-check email address (username is case-sensitive)
- Copy/paste temporary password from welcome email (avoid typos)
- Contact Chapter Secretary if still unable to login: secretary@cvma20-7.org

**Problem: "Password expired"**
- Temporary passwords expire after 7 days
- Contact Chapter Secretary to reset: secretary@cvma20-7.org

**Problem: "Account locked"**
- After 5 failed login attempts, account locks for security
- Wait 30 minutes and try again, OR
- Contact Chapter Secretary to unlock: secretary@cvma20-7.org

**Problem: Didn't receive welcome email**
- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify email address on membership application was correct
- Contact Chapter Secretary: secretary@cvma20-7.org

## 👤 **Step 2: Complete Your Member Profile**

Your member profile helps other chapter members connect with you and ensures you receive important communications.

### **Access Your Profile:**

1. Click **Profile** icon (top-right corner, person silhouette)

### **Profile Sections to Complete:**

- ✅ **First Name:** Your preferred first name
- ✅ **Last Name:** Your last name
- ✅ **Email:** Your primary email (already set during registration)
- ✅ **Phone:** Mobile number for chapter communications

## 📅 **Step 3: View Chapter Calendar & RSVP for Events**

Stay connected with chapter activities by viewing the event calendar and RSVP'ing for rides, meetings, and social events.

### **Access Chapter Calendar:**

1. From home page, click **Events** in navigation menu
2. OR click **Calendar** tile on home page
3. Calendar displays all upcoming chapter events

### **Calendar Views:**

- **Month View:** See all events for the month at a glance
- **Week View:** Detailed weekly schedule
- **Day View:** Hourly breakdown of events on a specific day
- **List View:** Upcoming events in chronological order

**Navigation:**
- Click **< Previous** or **Next >** to change months/weeks
- Click **Today** to return to current date
- Click event name to see full details

---

### **RSVP for an Event:**

1. **Find Event on Calendar**
   - Click event name in calendar
   - Event details page opens

2. **Review Event Details**
   - **Event Name:** Ride, meeting, social, fundraiser, etc.
   - **Date & Time:** Start and end times
   - **Location:** Destination and meeting point
   - **Description:** Event details, route info, special requirements
   - **Organizer:** CEB officer or member hosting event
   - **Current RSVPs:** See who else is attending

3. **RSVP Options**
   - **Attending:** Click green **RSVP Yes** button
   - **Maybe:** Click yellow **RSVP Maybe** button (you'll decide later)
   - **Not Attending:** No action needed (or click **RSVP No** if previously said yes)

4. **Add to Your Calendar** (Optional)
   - Click **Add to Calendar** button
   - Select calendar app: Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar
   - Event automatically syncs to your personal calendar

5. **Bring a Guest** (if applicable)
   - Some events allow guests (check event description)
   - Click **Add Guest** button
   - Enter guest name and email
   - Guest receives event details via email

---

### **Event Types & What to Expect:**

#### **🏍️ Chapter Rides**
- **Frequency:** 1-2 times per month
- **Duration:** 2-6 hours
- **What to Bring:** Motorcycle, riding gear, water, snacks
- **RSVP Required:** Yes (helps Road Captain plan route and stops)
- **Guests Allowed:** Yes (must have own motorcycle and proper gear)

**Ride Etiquette:**
- Arrive 15 minutes early for safety briefing
- Full tank of gas before departure
- Follow Road Captain's hand signals
- Ride in staggered formation
- No lane splitting or dangerous maneuvers

#### **📋 Chapter Meetings**
- **Frequency:** Monthly (typically first Thursday)
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Location:** Chapter meeting hall or designated location
- **RSVP Recommended:** Helps us plan seating and refreshments
- **Guests Allowed:** CVMA members only (unless special vote)

**What Happens at Meetings:**
- CEB reports (Commander, Treasurer, Secretary updates)
- Upcoming events planning
- New member introductions
- Voting on chapter business (Full Members only)
- Social time after meeting adjourns

#### **🎉 Social Events**
- **Examples:** BBQs, poker runs, fundraisers, holiday parties
- **Frequency:** Quarterly or as scheduled
- **RSVP Required:** Yes (food and seating planning)
- **Guests Allowed:** Yes (spouses, family, friends welcome)

#### **🎖️ Memorial & Veteran Support Events**
- **Examples:** Wreaths Across America, VA hospital visits, veteran funerals
- **Dress Code:** CVMA vest/colors or business casual
- **RSVP Required:** Yes (coordinate logistics)
- **Guests Allowed:** Varies by event (check description)

---

### **Manage Your RSVPs:**

1. Click **My Events** in navigation menu
2. See all events you've RSVP'd for
3. Filter by:
   - **Upcoming:** Events in the future
   - **Past:** Events you've attended
   - **My RSVPs:** Events you've said "Yes" or "Maybe"

4. **Change Your RSVP:**
   - Click event name
   - Click **Update RSVP** button
   - Select new response (Yes, Maybe, No)
   - Click **Save**

5. **Cancel RSVP:**
   - Click event name
   - Click **Cancel RSVP** button
   - Confirm cancellation
   - Road Captain or organizer is notified

**RSVP Etiquette:** If you RSVP "Yes" but can't attend, please update your RSVP as soon as possible (especially for rides and meals). This helps organizers plan accurately.

## 💰 **Step 7: Membership Dues & Financial Matters**

Understanding dues and financial obligations ensures your membership stays current.

### **Membership Dues:**

**Annual Dues:** $50/year (Chapter 20-7)
**National Dues:** $30/year (CVMA National)
**Total:** $80/year

**Due Date:** Anniversary of your membership start date
**Payment Methods:**
- Online via member portal (credit/debit card)
- Check mailed to Treasurer (payable to "CVMA Chapter 20-7")
- Cash at chapter meetings (receipt provided)

### **Check Your Dues Status:**

1. Click **Profile** → **My Profile**
2. Scroll to **Membership Information** section
3. View **Dues Status:**
   - ✅ **Current:** Dues paid through [date]
   - ⚠️ **Renewal Due:** Dues expire in [X] days
   - ❌ **Past Due:** Dues payment overdue

### **Pay Dues Online:**

1. Click **Pay Dues** button on profile page
2. Review amount due: $80 (or prorated amount)
3. Enter payment information:
   - Credit/debit card number
   - Expiration date
   - CVV code
   - Billing ZIP code
4. Click **Submit Payment**
5. Confirmation email sent to your email address
6. Receipt stored in **My Documents** section

### **Dues Renewal Reminders:**

You'll receive automatic email reminders:
- **60 days before due date:** "Dues renewal approaching"
- **30 days before due date:** "Dues renewal reminder"
- **7 days before due date:** "Dues renewal urgent"
- **Day after due date:** "Dues past due"

**Grace Period:** 30 days after due date before membership suspended

---

### **Financial Assistance:**

If you're experiencing financial hardship and cannot afford dues:

1. Contact **Chaplain** (confidential): chaplain@cvma20-7.org
2. Explain situation (job loss, medical emergency, etc.)
3. Chapter has hardship fund to assist members in need
4. Dues waiver or payment plan may be available

**Your membership matters more than the money.** We take care of our own.

## 🎖️ **Step 9: CVMA Culture & Brotherhood**

Understanding CVMA culture helps you integrate into the brotherhood and honor our mission: **"Vets Serving Vets"**.

### **Core Values:**

**1. Respect**
- Respect fellow members, regardless of rank, branch, or combat experience
- Respect the patch and what it represents
- Respect CEB decisions and chapter leadership

**2. Loyalty**
- Loyalty to CVMA mission and fellow members
- Support your brothers and sisters in times of need
- Defend the CVMA reputation and name

**3. Service**
- Serve fellow veterans through support programs
- Volunteer in your community
- Help other members without expectation of reward

**4. Integrity**
- Honesty in all dealings
- Keep commitments (RSVP's, volunteer duties, financial obligations)
- Report concerns to CEB rather than gossip

**5. Brotherhood/Sisterhood**
- We're family, not just an organization
- Support each other through tough times
- Celebrate successes together

---

### **Patch Etiquette:**

**Full Member Patch:**
- Full Members wear complete CVMA patch (top rocker, center patch, bottom rocker)
- Earned after 6-month probationary period and chapter vote

**Supporter Patch:**
- Supporters wear Supporter patch (no rockers)
- Still valued members of CVMA family

**Associate Patch:**
- Associates (non-combat veterans) wear Associate patch
- Important contributors to chapter success

**Patch Care:**
- Treat patch with respect (it represents all CVMA members)
- Never let patch touch ground
- Clean and maintain vest/patch regularly
- No unauthorized modifications

**Patch Protocol:**
- Don't loan patch to anyone
- Report lost/stolen patch to CEB immediately
- Return patch if leaving CVMA (voluntary or involuntary)

---

### **Meeting & Event Protocol:**

**Respect for Process:**
- Chapter meetings follow Robert's Rules of Order
- Wait to be recognized by Commander before speaking
- Full Members only vote on chapter business
- Guests/Supporters observe unless invited to speak

**Mobile Phones:**
- Silence phones during meetings
- Emergency calls only (step outside)
- No texting during Commander or CEB reports

**Alcohol & Substances:**
- No alcohol at chapter meetings
- Drinking at social events: moderation and responsibility
- ZERO TOLERANCE for riding under the influence
- Drugs prohibited at all CVMA events

**Conflict Resolution:**
- Disagreements happen - address respectfully
- Take serious conflicts to CEB (don't spread drama)
- Commander has final say on chapter matters
- Appeals process available (see Bylaws Appendix C)
