## 🎯 **Training Overview for CEB Officers**

This comprehensive guide covers all aspects of the CVMA Chapter 20-7 Salesforce platform specifically designed for **Chapter Executive Board (CEB) Officers**.

### **What You'll Learn:**
- Platform navigation and officer dashboard usage
- Member management and lifecycle oversight
- Event planning and coordination tools
- Financial oversight and reporting capabilities
- Communication and outreach management

---

## 📊 **CEB Officer Dashboard Training**

### **Accessing Your Dashboard**
1. **Login Process**: Use your assigned CVMA credentials
2. **Officer View**: Your dashboard shows chapter-wide metrics
3. **Navigation**: Key areas accessible from the main menu
4. **Mobile Access**: Full functionality available on mobile devices

### **Key Dashboard Components**
- **Member Overview**: Current membership status and trends
- **Event Management**: Upcoming events and RSVP tracking
- **Financial Summary**: Chapter financial health indicators
- **Communication Hub**: Member outreach and messaging tools

### **Daily Tasks Checklist**
- ✅ Review new member applications
- ✅ Check event attendance updates
- ✅ Monitor financial transactions
- ✅ Respond to member communications

## 👥 **Member Management Procedures**

### **New Member Onboarding**
1. **Application Review**: Validate membership applications
2. **Background Verification**: Coordinate required checks
3. **Welcome Process**: Initiate automated welcome sequence
4. **Mentorship Assignment**: Connect new members with mentors

### **Member Lifecycle Management**
- **Status Updates**: Active, Inactive, Suspended member management
- **Dues Tracking**: Monitor payment status and follow up
- **Event Participation**: Track member engagement levels
- **Recognition Programs**: Manage awards and acknowledgments

### **Special Situations**
- **Deployment Support**: Automatic dues suspension for military deployment
- **Hardship Assistance**: Process benevolent fund requests
- **Disciplinary Actions**: Follow formal investigation procedures
- **Member Retention**: Early intervention for at-risk members

## 📅 **Event Coordination Training**

### **Event Planning Process**
1. **Event Creation**: Set up new chapter events in the system
2. **RSVP Management**: Track member responses and attendance
3. **Resource Planning**: Coordinate venues, supplies, and logistics
4. **Communication**: Send event updates and reminders

### **Event Types and Management**
- **Chapter Meetings**: Regular monthly business meetings
- **Social Events**: Rides, gatherings, and community activities
- **Fundraisers**: Charity events and revenue generation
- **Memorial Services**: Honor fallen veterans and members

### **Post-Event Procedures**
- **Attendance Recording**: Update member participation records
- **Financial Reconciliation**: Process event-related expenses and income
- **Feedback Collection**: Gather member input for improvement
- **Photo and Documentation**: Archive event memories and achievements

## 💰 **Financial Oversight Training**

### **Financial Dashboard Overview**
- **Revenue Tracking**: Dues, donations, and fundraising income
- **Expense Management**: Operating costs and approved expenditures
- **Budget Monitoring**: Compare actual vs. planned spending
- **Compliance Reporting**: Ensure IRS and regulatory compliance

### **Key Financial Responsibilities**
1. **Monthly Reviews**: Analyze financial statements and trends
2. **Approval Workflows**: Process expense requests and approvals
3. **Audit Preparation**: Maintain records for annual audits
4. **Transparency**: Ensure member access to appropriate financial information

### **Emergency Financial Procedures**
- **Benevolent Fund**: Process member assistance requests
- **Emergency Expenses**: Handle urgent chapter needs
- **Fraud Prevention**: Monitor for suspicious financial activity
- **Crisis Management**: Financial decision-making during emergencies
