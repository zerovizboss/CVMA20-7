# Epic #8 Guest User & Campaign Member Access Guide
## Public Features and Limited Access for CVMA Chapter 20-7

*Making CVMA Resources Accessible to All Veterans*

---

## Overview

Epic #8 provides **tiered access** to ensure both security and accessibility. This guide explains what features are available to different user types without requiring full member login.

---

## Access Levels Explained

### 🔓 **Public Access (No Login Required)**
Available to anyone visiting the CVMA Chapter 20-7 website

### 👤 **Guest User Access**
Limited features available with guest login (no membership required)

### 🎯 **Campaign Member Access**
Enhanced access for fundraising participants and event supporters

### 🏍️ **Full Member Access**
Complete access to all Epic #8 features (requires active CVMA membership)

## Phase 2: Einstein Analytics - Public Features

### What's Available Without Login

#### 📊 **Public Chapter Statistics**
- **Member growth trends** (anonymized data)
- **Event attendance statistics**
- **Fundraising campaign progress**
- **Community impact metrics**

**How to Access:**
1. Visit cvma20-7-dev-ed.develop.my.site.com
2. Navigate to "Chapter Analytics"
3. View public dashboard (no login required)

#### 📈 **Campaign Performance Tracking**
- **Active fundraising campaigns** and their progress
- **Event registration numbers** for public events
- **Community service impact metrics**
- **Chapter achievement highlights**

### Guest User Features (Free Registration)

#### 🔍 **Enhanced Analytics Access**
- **Historical trend data** (last 12 months)
- **Event calendar** with predictive attendance
- **Fundraising goal tracking** for all public campaigns
- **Chapter comparison metrics** (vs. regional averages)

**How to Get Guest Access:**
1. Visit the CVMA Chapter 20-7 site
2. Click "Guest Registration" (no membership fees)
3. Provide basic contact information
4. Verify email address
5. Access expanded analytics features

## Campaign Member Access

### Who Qualifies
- **Fundraising participants** (any donation amount)
- **Event volunteers**
- **Ride participants** (registered for chapter events)
- **Community service volunteers**
- **Veteran family members** and supporters

### Enhanced Features Available

#### 📊 **Campaign Analytics Dashboard**
- **Real-time progress tracking** for campaigns you support
- **Impact measurement** showing your contribution effects
- **Team performance** if participating in group campaigns
- **Historical participation** records and achievements

#### 🤖 **Automated Updates**
- **Personalized progress reports** via email
- **Campaign milestone notifications**
- **Event reminders** and updates
- **Thank you messages** with impact details

#### 🌐 **Cross-Chapter Collaboration**
- **Participate in multi-chapter events** as a supporter
- **Access coordination tools** for events you're registered for
- **Connect with other campaign members** across chapters
- **Share campaign progress** on social media

### How to Become a Campaign Member

#### Option 1: Donation Participation
1. **Make any donation** to a CVMA Chapter 20-7 campaign
2. **Provide contact information** during donation process
3. **Verify email address**
4. **Automatic campaign member access** activated

#### Option 2: Event Participation
1. **Register for any chapter event** (rides, meetings, fundraisers)
2. **Complete registration form**
3. **Receive confirmation email** with access instructions
4. **Login using event registration credentials**

#### Option 3: Volunteer Registration
1. **Sign up for volunteer opportunities**
2. **Complete volunteer background check** (if required)
3. **Attend volunteer orientation**
4. **Receive volunteer access credentials**

## Security and Privacy

### Data Protection
- **No personal member data** exposed to public or guest users
- **Anonymized analytics** protect individual privacy
- **Secure guest registration** with email verification
- **Campaign member data** limited to participation history

### What's NOT Available to Non-Members
- **Individual member profiles** and personal information
- **Internal chapter communications** and decision-making data
- **Detailed financial records** beyond public campaign information
- **Advanced predictive analytics** and strategic planning tools
- **Direct member contact** information

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I access Epic #8 features without being a CVMA member?
**A:** Yes! Many features are available to:
- **Public users** (no registration needed)
- **Guest users** (free registration)
- **Campaign members** (participants in events/fundraising)

### Q: What's the difference between guest access and campaign member access?
**A:**
- **Guest access**: Basic enhanced features with registration
- **Campaign member**: Additional features when you participate in/support campaigns

### Q: How do I upgrade from guest to campaign member?
**A:** Simply participate in any:
- Fundraising campaign (any donation amount)
- Chapter event registration
- Volunteer opportunity

### Q: Can I access VA benefits information without any registration?
**A:** Yes! VA benefits information and crisis support resources are **completely public** - no registration required.

### Q: Is my information secure as a guest user?
**A:** Absolutely! We use the same security protocols as full members, and you'll only see anonymized/public data.

### Q: Can campaign members participate in cross-chapter events?
**A:** Yes! Campaign members can register for and participate in multi-chapter rides and events.

### For Full Epic #8 Access
1. **Contact** CVMA Chapter 20-7 for membership information
2. **Complete** membership application process
3. **Receive** full member credentials
4. **Access** complete Epic #8 feature set
