## 🎯 **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

### **General Questions**

**Q: Is the Veterans Resources Portal free to use?**
A: Yes, the portal is completely free for all users. CVMA provides this service as part of our "Vets Serving Vets" mission.

**Q: Do I need to be a CVMA member to access resources?**
A: No, the portal is available to all veterans and the public. CVMA membership provides enhanced features like personalized recommendations and eligibility checking.

**Q: How often is the organization information updated?**
A: We update organization information quarterly and verify contact details regularly. If you find outdated information, please let us know.

**Q: Can family members use the portal?**
A: Yes, many veteran service organizations also serve family members, and the portal includes programs for military spouses, children, and caregivers.

### **Technical Questions**

**Q: Why can't I access eligibility checking as a guest user?**
A: Detailed eligibility checking requires member authentication to provide accurate, personalized assessments based on your service history and current status.

**Q: How do I reset my CVMA member password?**
A: Contact CVMA member services or use the password reset link on the login page.

**Q: Is my personal information secure?**
A: Yes, we use government-grade security measures and never share personal information with external organizations without your consent.

### **Application Questions**

**Q: Can the portal help me apply for benefits?**
A: The portal provides direct links to organization applications and step-by-step guidance. CVMA members receive additional application assistance.

**Q: How do I track my applications?**
A: CVMA members can use the application tracking feature to monitor progress across multiple organizations from one dashboard.

**Q: What if I'm denied by an organization?**
A: The portal includes alternative organizations for each service category, and CVMA member services can help identify additional resources.
